# Companion review record

Use this structure for the working record beside the policy. It is separate from the policy intended for adoption. Keep it updated across questions, revisions, and the final writing pass. Replace field descriptions with actual information; an unknown stays visibly unresolved.

## Status and inputs

- Status: incomplete or complete under the skill's completion criteria.
- Policy artifact: path and format.
- Template: exact path or resource identifier, selected name, and supplied version information.
- Source documents: files consulted, with supplied version information where available.
- User answers: stable answer identifiers with the question and explicit answer, or a conversation reference that remains accessible. Preserve the scope of corrections and decisions.

## Coverage and provenance

One row per inventoried requirement, including substantive author instructions and conditional alternatives. Add rows for distinct claims or commitments where necessary to make their support inspectable. Keep all requirements in the record, including unresolved ones.

| ID | Template location and requirement | Source passage or user-answer ID | Policy location | Resolution or open gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Record exact headings, page numbers, table cells, or paragraph excerpts sufficient to find supporting material again. Explain conditional selections and confirmed inapplicability. For template-only commitments, link the user's confirmation. Keep adopted requirements distinct from evidence that a control is operating.

Source paths and provenance links belong in this internal record and `policy-map.md`, never in the policy uploaded to Vanta. These working records are separate from the policy deliverable.

## References to other Vanta policies

| Policy location | Referenced policy's exact title | Evidence of Vanta availability or explicit upload confirmation | Verified Vanta URL or identifier, if available | Resolution or pending question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Account for every policy reference, including plain-text titles. Distinguish a policy already in Vanta from one explicitly confirmed for upload with the generated policy set. A local file or template is not availability evidence. Keep uncertain references linked to `QQQ:` markers and second-pass questions. Record when supported content was incorporated directly to remove a source dependency. If there are no references to other policies, say so.

## Open questions

| Question ID | Requirement or policy location | Uncertainty and question asked | Answer or pending status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

During the first pass, link each visible `QQQ:` marker to its policy location, requirement, source context, and pending question. On the second pass, reconcile a scan of the saved policy with this record and investigate every marker before escalating it. Record the files and sections read, searches and alternate terms used, cross-references followed, and evidence found. Resolve source-answerable markers with exact provenance; for remaining questions, explain the specific missing fact, unresolved conflict, access blocker, or decision that requires the user. Ask only those remaining questions through the user-question tool. A shared question may cover repeated markers only when every affected location is tracked. Record any guidance or proposed options separately from verified facts, then preserve the user's explicit answer and decision scope as provenance. Keep unanswered questions and their policy markers pending. For a missing template or another input that prevents locating the gap in a policy, record the blocker here and report it directly.

## Additions beyond the template

| Addition and policy location | Why it helps | Source passage or user confirmation | Included or proposed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Distinguish included content from optional suggestions awaiting a decision. If there are no additions, say so. Notify the user of included additions outside the policy, without inserting editorial labels in its text.

## Final checks

Record the actual outcome and remaining blockers for template coverage, source support, resolved questions, legibility, consistency, conciseness, and the search for unresolved content. Include the review for standalone use in Vanta: no original-documentation or template dependencies, with every remaining policy reference supported by Vanta availability evidence or explicit upload confirmation. Identify the humanize-writing pass and verification performed after it, including the repeated reference check. Checks not yet performed remain pending. Document completeness, organizational approval, and control implementation are separate statuses.

# Companion review record

Use this structure for the working record beside the policy. It is separate from the policy intended for adoption. Keep it updated across questions, revisions, and the final writing pass. Replace field descriptions with actual information; an unknown stays visibly unresolved.

## Status and inputs

- Status: incomplete or complete under the skill's completion criteria.
- Policy artifact: path and format.
- Template: exact path or resource identifier, selected name, and supplied version information.
- Source documents: files consulted, with supplied version information where available.
- User answers: stable answer identifiers with the question and explicit answer, or a conversation reference that remains accessible. Preserve the scope of corrections and decisions.

## Coverage and provenance

One row per inventoried requirement, including substantive author instructions and conditional alternatives. Add rows for distinct claims or commitments where necessary to make their support inspectable. Keep all requirements in the record, including unresolved ones.

| ID | Template location and requirement | Source passage or user-answer ID | Policy location | Resolution or open gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Record exact headings, page numbers, table cells, or paragraph excerpts sufficient to find supporting material again. Explain conditional selections and confirmed inapplicability. For template-only commitments, link the user's confirmation. Keep adopted requirements distinct from evidence that a control is operating.

## Open questions

| Question ID | Requirement or policy location | Uncertainty and question asked | Answer or pending status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Each unresolved policy location has a visible `QQQ:` line in the draft. For a missing template or another input that prevents locating the gap in a policy, record the blocker here and report it directly. Preserve resolved answers as provenance, and make pending items easy to find.

## Additions beyond the template

| Addition and policy location | Why it helps | Source passage or user confirmation | Included or proposed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Distinguish included content from optional suggestions awaiting a decision. If there are no additions, say so. Notify the user of included additions outside the policy, without inserting editorial labels in its text.

## Final checks

Record the actual outcome and remaining blockers for template coverage, source support, resolved questions, legibility, consistency, conciseness, and the search for unresolved content. Identify the humanize-writing pass and verification performed after it. Checks not yet performed remain pending. Document completeness, organizational approval, and control implementation are separate statuses.
